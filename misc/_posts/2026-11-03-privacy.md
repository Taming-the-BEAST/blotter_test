### Privacy policy

Taming the BEAST uses a self-hosted [Matomo](https://matomo.org) instance for web analytics, that is, statistics on how the site is used: number of visits, pages viewed, links followed and approximate visitor location.
We use these statistics in aggregate to see which tutorials are read and to improve the site.
They are not used for advertising or to identify individual visitors.

For each page view, Matomo records the page address and referring page, outgoing links and downloads, the time of the visit, browser, operating system, screen size and language, and a masked IP address, anonymised by removing its last two bytes and used to estimate location.
Matomo may set cookies to recognise repeat visits.

Matomo runs exclusively on servers operated by the [SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics](https://www.sib.swiss), and the data is not sold or shared with third parties.

We follow the [SIB privacy policy](https://www.sib.swiss/privacy-policy), which also describes your rights regarding your data and how to contact SIB about them.
